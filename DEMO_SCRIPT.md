# 3-4 minute demo video script (screen record + talk)

**Before you record:** open the site, wait until it loads, press **Reset** (or call `POST /api/reset`), make sure the badge says "Gemini AI live". Record screen + microphone at 1080p. Do one practice run first.

**0:00 - Hook with real numbers (20s)**  "Last year 96% of companies had a supply-chain disruption, costing about 4 million dollars on average. Finding the problem takes 8.7 hours and understanding it takes over 40 more. Only 15% of companies can coordinate AI across departments. OpsPilot closes that gap: a crew of seven AI agents that handles the whole crisis in about 25 seconds, with a human approving the risky parts."


**0:20 - Login (10s)**  Click "Try the live demo account".

**0:30 - Command Center (20s)**  "Here is our company's data: orders, suppliers, stock. Let's report a disruption." Open "Risks it found before you did" (the Sentinel agent scans weather, ports and suppliers on its own), then click **Let the crew handle it** on the Port congestion card (or click **Port strike delays shipments** below).

**0:45 - Watch the agents (60s)** Talk while the timeline streams:
- Planner: "It understood the incident and made a 6-step plan."
- Analyst: "It found 7 orders short on stock and ₹2 lakh at risk."
- Sourcing: "It compared alternate suppliers and picked one that arrives in time, explaining why."
- Comms: "It drafted personalised emails."
- Guardian: "Policy rules - not AI - flag risky actions."

**1:45 - Human in the loop (30s)**  "The Guardian paused. Purchase orders above ₹50,000 and enterprise emails need my approval." Open **Preview email**, reject one item, click **Confirm my decisions**.

**2:15 - Execution + impact (40s)**  "The Executor placed purchase orders, updated orders, sent emails and opened an escalation ticket." Point to the **Business impact** card: orders saved, revenue protected, hours saved.

**2:55 - Proof (25s)**  Click **See what changed in the systems**: Orders statuses, Purchase Orders, Email Outbox, Tickets.

**3:20 - Close (20s)**  "Planning, reasoning, tool use, collaboration, action, and human oversight: OpsPilot is agentic AI that actually does the work. Thank you."

**Bonus (if time allows, 30s)**  Open **Ask the crew** (bottom right), click the mic and say "What is my biggest risk?". Then open **Insights** for the charts, and on a finished run click **Download report** and **Read briefing aloud**.
